# Devices

How to keep your laptop safe.

- [DV-1](../sources/device-standard.md#dv-1): Your laptop uses full-disk encryption: FileVault on macOS, BitLocker on Windows.
- [DV-2](../sources/device-standard.md#dv-2): The screen locks after 5 minutes without input.
- [DV-3](../sources/device-standard.md#dv-3): Install security updates within 7 days of release.
- [DV-4](../sources/device-standard.md#dv-4): Reach internal systems only through the company [VPN](glossary.md#vpn). Split tunneling is not allowed.
