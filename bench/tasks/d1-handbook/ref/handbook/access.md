# Access

How you sign in and who can reach what.

- [AC-1](../sources/access-policy.md#ac-1): Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. A passphrase is easiest.
- [AC-2](../sources/access-policy.md#ac-2): Turn on [MFA](glossary.md#mfa) everywhere it is offered. A hardware security key is the preferred second factor, and SMS codes are not allowed.
- [AC-3](../sources/access-policy.md#ac-3): Your manager reviews your team's access every 90 days and removes what is no longer needed.
- [AC-4](../sources/access-policy.md#ac-4): Internal apps sign in through [SSO](glossary.md#sso). When an app joins SSO, its local accounts are disabled within 24 hours.
