# Incidents

What to do when something looks wrong.

- [IN-1](../sources/incident-runbook.md#in-1): Report a suspected security incident within 1 hour in #security-incidents, even if you are not sure.
- [IN-2](../sources/incident-runbook.md#in-2): The on-call security engineer becomes the [incident commander](glossary.md#incident-commander) and makes the decisions until the incident is closed.
- [IN-3](../sources/incident-runbook.md#in-3): Do not wipe or reimage an affected device until the incident commander says so.
- [IN-4](../sources/incident-runbook.md#in-4): A blameless post-incident review is held within 5 business days of closing the incident.
