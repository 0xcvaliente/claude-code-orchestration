# Glossary

## MFA
Multi-factor authentication: signing in with a second proof of identity, such as a hardware key, on top of your password.

## SSO
Single sign-on: one company login that opens every connected internal app.

## VPN
Virtual private network: the encrypted connection that is the only way into internal systems from outside.

## PII
Personally identifiable information: personal data that can identify a specific person.

## Incident commander
The person who leads the response to a security incident and makes its decisions until it is closed.
