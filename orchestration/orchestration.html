Claude Code orchestration

A trigger starts an orchestrator that plans, dispatches cheap explorer and mid-tier writer workers, gates results through a fresh-context verifier, and either ships or retries with feedback.

On narrow screens, scroll the diagram horizontally. The relationships and evidence are also listed below.

Notes and assumptions

  • Design proposal, not an observed system: every element is proposed.
  • Solid arrows are control flow; dashed arrows are returned results, retries, and logging.
  • Model tiers are roles (cheap, mid-tier, strongest); pick concrete models per role after measuring.
  • Retry limits, timeouts, and the merge policy are unspecified.
