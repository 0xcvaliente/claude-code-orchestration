Orchestration benchmark

Generated by bench/report.mjs from lite, lite-k2, sweeps on 2026-10-15. Model usage is counted in tokens only. Markdown version: benchmarks.md.

Headline findings

  • H1 k2 at the shipped limit: orchestrators close to a plain Opus session and faster: not supported. O-mix -20.4 points, 8.1 min vs 10.7 min; O-O -12.6 points, 13.3 min vs 10.7 min.
    On k2-stdlib-toolkit-xl (main), O-mix and O-O each reach a final score within 5 points of P-O with lower median wall time.
  • H2 Small tasks: orchestration uses 1.5x or more tokens for no pass-rate gain: not supported. t1-parallel O-mix/P-S 1.19x tokens, pass 100% vs 100%; t1-parallel O-O/P-O 2.06x tokens, pass 100% vs 100%; t2-single O-mix/P-S 1.31x tokens, pass 100% vs 100%; t2-single O-O/P-O 1.46x tokens, pass 100% vs 100%.
    On small tasks (t1, t2), O-mix uses at least 1.5x the median tokens of P-S and O-O at least 1.5x P-O, with no pass-rate gain.
  • H4 False successes, orchestrated vs plain (main cells): supported. 0/28 vs 1/28.
    Pooled over main-cell runs, the false-success rate of O-mix+O-O is lower than that of P-S+P-O.
  • H5 AUTO within 10% of the best setup's score per million tokens on two-thirds of the tasks with data: not supported. Within on 1 of 7 tasks with data.
    Efficiency (mean score pct per million median tokens): AUTO is within 10% of the best arm on at least two-thirds of the tasks with data.
  • H6 Orchestration alone (O-O vs P-O) holds its score on large tasks: not supported. t1-parallel 0 points, 2.06x tokens; t2-single 0 points, 1.46x tokens; h1-query-engine 0 points, 1.78x tokens; h2-scheduler 0 points, 1.16x tokens; k1-stdlib-toolkit -0.5 points, 0.99x tokens; h3-sql-engine 0 points, 0.74x tokens; k2-stdlib-toolkit-xl -12.6 points, 0.74x tokens.
    O-O vs P-O (orchestration alone): O-O scores at least as high as P-O on every large task.
  • H7 The orchestrator's lead grows with time, and P-O wins under token caps: not supported. k2-stdlib-toolkit-xl: cap10 -14.6, main -20.4, cap60 +1.
    On k2-stdlib-toolkit-xl, the O-mix minus P-O score difference grows from cap10 to main to cap60; at the two budget cells P-O scores at least as high as O-mix.

Method

  • Each setup works on the task text alone; hidden tests the agent never sees grade a snapshot during the run and the final tree. Score is passed / total hidden tests; a run passes when every hidden test passes.
  • t90 and t100 are the first graded snapshot at 90% and 100%. AUC is the time-weighted mean score over the time limit, the timeline read as a step function from 0.
  • A false success is a run whose final message claims success (bench/lib/claims.mjs) while the hidden grade fails.
  • k2 has two scores. The strict score is the official one: the task forbids importing any oracle module, so a toolkit module that imports re (the regex oracle) fails to import and scores 0. The lenient score is a secondary variant that lets every module except toolkit/regex.py import re, with the regex cases watched exactly as in the strict grade; it shows capability without that one rule. Hypotheses use the strict score.
